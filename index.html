Geopolitics — the live map

BTC is not trading like wartime gold this cycle. Oil shocks first hit inflation → yields → Fed → liquidity, and BTC behaves as a high-beta risk asset. Only later, if the dollar-weaponization story dominates, does the sovereign / debasement bid show up. Gold ~$4,350 vs BTC’s still-deep drawdown is that split in one chart.

Hot files this week. Trump–Xi in Washington on 24 Sep (trade truce, $14B Taiwan arms package used as a chip, Iran/Hormuz pressure). UNGA same week. Trump signed H.R. 5334 on 18 Sep — 30-day clock to 18 Oct for up to 500% tariffs on Russian goods and up to 100% on the largest remaining buyers of Russian energy. Hormuz flows still ~7.6 mbpd vs ~20.7 pre-war. WTI ~$94 / Brent ~$102. Ukraine hit a Moscow refinery after a Russian strike on an Odesa cargo ship. Houthis claimed a Riyadh missile strike and weekend Hormuz traffic dropped again.

Transmission, in one line

War → oil → CPI → Fed → 10-year → ETF flows → BTC. That is the 2026 path that already took BTC from $126k to the $58k June low. Inverse: détente → oil down → hike odds off → shorts cover IBIT → $90k+. Do not size as if BTC is gold on day one of a Hormuz closure.
